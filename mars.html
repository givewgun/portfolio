WHY THIS IS HERE

Windows, margins, and landings

Wait for the window

A Hohmann transfer is the cheapest path between two orbits, but only when the geometry is right. Good architecture has windows too: the moment a migration is cheap is rarely the moment someone asks for it.

Budget the margin

Every gram of propellant is decided before launch. Identity platforms serving millions and IoT backends going from 20,000 to 5 million devices get the same treatment: size it, test it, then keep reserve.

Stick the landing

Shipping is the landing burn. Plans are cheap; the last 50 metres are where the engineering shows.
